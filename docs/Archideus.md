Archideus is a multiplayer city building game set in ancient Greece during the Classical Period (480–323 BC) where you play as Archideus, the prospective god of architecture. 
## Game Design
Archideus is a grid-based city builder where the players work together to build up a Greek city. The players have to balance multiple resources to keep their settlement working such as [Labour](Labour.md), [Food](Food.md), [Wood](Wood.md), [Stone](Stone.md), [Metal](Metal.md), [Drachma](Drachma.md) and [Faith](Faith.md). 
The game starts with an unpopulated map containing forests, iron and stone deposits scattered around it. With the starting capital of X Food, Y Wood, Z Stone, and 100 Drachmas, the players are free to start zoning plots for [[Oikos]] to allow some families to move in. As families join the settlement they start building their oikos themselves and once they've constructed their house they are available as [Labour](Labour.md) which is used when constructing other buildings such as the [[Farmhouse]]. The labour is also used for working the farmhouse which produces a steady income of [Food](Food.md) based on the number of [Farms](Farm.md) around it.
### Objective
The deities of ancient Greece are petty and easily become jealous of other gods. As such, a newly born god is sure to be kept subdued if noticed by the greater deities. In Archideus, your goal is to slowly expand your influence over mankind without being noticed by the other gods. As you gain followers to worship you and devote themselves to architecture and civil engineering, you also grow and unlock new powers. Can you become powerful enough to face Athena and earn your seat in Olympus?
